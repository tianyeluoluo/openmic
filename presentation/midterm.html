Humor in the whole story

For this project, I treat a joke as a coherent story.

  1. Everyday situation

    What the character wants
    and expects to happen

  2. Character response

    A choice, misunderstanding
    or believable inner thought

  3. Changed expectation

    A surprising consequence
    that the audience can follow

Optimizing each small beat in isolation can make the whole experience feel artificial.

Read motivation, cause, consequence and rhythm together.

Working approach developed through iterative script review

CFunSet-guided prompt engineering

Retrieve useful training examples. Extract what the listener expects and what changes that understanding.

Role prompts

Give planning, writing and review different responsibilities.

Structured planning

Use a story spine or a funny moment. Keep the writer free to improve weak ideas.

Critique and revision

Identify a weak line and repair the action, reply or ending that causes it.

Summary of the writing guidance

Keep a coherent experience and believable motives. Let actions or replies shift expectations. Keep necessary setup and trim repeated explanations.

Adaptation preserves the source mechanism. Original writing transfers it into a new situation.

Implementation: humor_data.py, humor_generation.py and humor_craft.py · Retrieval and prompts, without generator fine-tuning

Four core evaluation dimensions

Pro applies a prompted 1–5 rubric to the full script.

DimensionScoring criteria
HumorA specific, surprising yet understandable turn or response
Cultural fitChinese context, audience knowledge and natural social behavior
Spoken naturalnessIdiomatic phrasing that sounds natural when spoken aloud
StructureWhole-story coherence, reveal timing and useful callbacks
Blocking issues require an original quote, a reason and a repair.

Humor: 3 = understandable but weak. 4 = a specific comic turn. Realism has a separate check. Human calibration remains necessary.

Prompted scores, distinct from CFunSet binary labels · Implementation: humor_quality_loop.py
